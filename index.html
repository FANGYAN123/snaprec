Full-page screenshots & tab screen recording, right in Chrome.

SnapRec scrolls and stitches the entire page into one image — including infinite-scroll feeds — and records the current tab to a video file. Everything is processed locally in your browser.

Install from Chrome Web Store See pricing

What it does

Full-page screenshot

Automatically scrolls the page, waits for lazy-loaded content and stitches everything into a single PNG. Press Esc anytime to stop and save what has been captured.

Tab screen recording

Records the current tab with optional tab audio, saved as a WebM video in your downloads folder. The free plan includes 60 seconds per recording.

Private by design

Screenshots and recordings never leave your computer. No account, no uploads, no tracking. The only network request is a license validation check.
